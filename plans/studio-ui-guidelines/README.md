## Spec-wide decisions

- **Where this spec restates the kit's contract, the reconcile corrects the restatement or reduces it to a pointer at `ui.md`.** That covers the tokens (§1.1), the font stacks and type scale (§2.1, §2.2), the accordion's look (§5.2) and the space scale (§7). Which of the two each stub chooses is a detail-phase decision, taken in the same pull request as `ui.md`'s own detailing.
- **Dead rules go in the pull request that stops the spec naming them.** `.style-row` and `.set-dot` in packages/studio/styles/inspector.css ride with `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/form-row-part-vocabulary`; `.layer-row.dragging`, `.layer-row.drop-target` and `.file-tree-item.dragging` in packages/studio/styles/panels.css with `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/moves-without-dragging`. `.empty-state*` in panels.css and overlays.css is already acknowledged as emitter-less by §11.2 and is left to whoever next touches those sheets.
- **§14's Standards Alignment notes are not markers.** The WCAG 2.2 row's "cut/paste is the stated alternative to every drag" and the ATAG row's "keyboard alternative to every drag" overclaim until `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/moves-without-dragging` lands, for all four drags §8.2 names (including tab order and grid column order, or the recorded reason one of them is out of scope); the census leaves them, and that plan re-reads them.
- **Open item or editorial drift.** A sentence that states what Studio draws or does, or a procedure a contributor follows, and that the code contradicts, is an open item: it gets a marker and a plan (§1.1's pipeline, §5.2's accordion, §8.1's highlights). A citation whose target moved while the statement still holds, an explanatory aside whose conclusion still holds, a misplaced bullet, or an enumeration that is true as far as it goes is editorial drift: it is recorded above and fixed by whichever plan next edits the section, or by a patch release.
- **§10 is a summary, owned by a plan that requires each summarised section's owner.** `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/conventions-checklist` claims §10 and requires the nine plans whose sections its failing items restate: `plan:_shared/studio-state-contract` (§9.1), `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/debounce-draft-layer` (§4.4), `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/form-row-part-vocabulary` (§4.1), `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/label-abbreviations` (§2.3), `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/empty-state-copy` (§11), `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/unrendered-placements` (§12.1), `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/name-and-chord-gaps` (§12.3), `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/git-panel-action-list` (§12.5), and the enabling `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/conventions-checklist-controls` for the four items only §10 states. No one of them can remove §10's marker while another item is still false.
- **§10's marker shrinks as its sections land; its items are one plan's.** A plan that closes an item §10's marker names deletes that clause and recounts "Five inherit" in the same pull request (`plan:studio-ui-guidelines/debounce-draft-layer`, `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/empty-state-copy`, `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/git-panel-action-list`, `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/name-and-chord-gaps`; `plan:_shared/studio-state-contract` should do the same for §9.1's clause), so the marker never names a gap the tree no longer has. The checklist items' wording, and the marker's final replacement, are `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/conventions-checklist`'s alone, quoting each section as it landed.
- **A sentence several plans edit is recounted or rebased, never assumed.** §12.4's "Thirteen modules" (`plan:studio-ui-guidelines/git-panel-action-list` and `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/empty-state-copy` each drop one; `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/run-reported-sweep` replaces the marker whole); §5.1's example (`plan:studio-ui-guidelines/accordion-styling-facts` drops `size` and fixes the `ui.md` citation, `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/section-open-state` drops the `open` line); §4.5 (`plan:studio-ui-guidelines/debounce-draft-layer` rewrites it whole, so `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/multiline-text-field` skips its clause if second); §7's form-row cell (`plan:studio-ui-guidelines/spacing-scale` names §4.1, so `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/form-row-part-vocabulary` skips its rename if second); §9.3's "Module-local state" sentence (whichever of `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/section-open-state` and `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/retire-renderer-registry` lands first rewrites it, in the words the latter gives). `packages/studio/scripts/check-styles.ts` is edited by `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/token-pipeline-prose`, `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/shell-layout-prose` and `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/conventions-checklist-controls`; they add independent rules and the second rebases.
- **What a command record prints has one home: `packages/studio/src/commands/projection.ts`.** `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/git-panel-action-list` creates it (`commandControl`, `commandTooltip` in GPA1.1, `commandRow` in GPA1.2), `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/name-and-chord-gaps` extends it (`placementRows`, `doorControl`), and `plan:studio/file-tree-command-records` consumes it. `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/empty-state-copy`'s `commandEmptyAction` is the empty-state vocabulary's adapter over the same registry calls, with one stated difference (no hint for an enabled, chordless action); whichever of it and GPA1.1 lands second may build one on the other. The pane menu's `paneMenuRows` (`plan:studio-ui-guidelines/unrendered-placements`) is the one projector outside it, because it answers for a pane rather than the focus. `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/commandbar-label-stripping`'s `menuRowsOf` maps `commandRow` once both have landed.
- **A refusal's Problems source is the surface's visible name, and `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/run-reported-sweep` owns the rule.** A site two plans convert takes that plan's name: "Canvas" for the derivation notice (with `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/empty-state-copy`), "Publish" for the deploy checklist (with `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/git-panel-action-list`), "Pane" for the pane menu's rows (with `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/unrendered-placements`). New sources follow the same rule: "Source Control" and "Welcome" (`plan:studio-ui-guidelines/git-panel-action-list`), "Bottom Dock" (`plan:studio-ui-guidelines/name-and-chord-gaps`), "Files", "Languages" and "Navigator" (`plan:studio-ui-guidelines/empty-state-copy`). None is a source a report clears wholesale.
- **A control a plan adds meets §10's control rules in either landing order.** `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/conventions-checklist-controls` sweeps every surface document for one accessible name, a live `aria-expanded` on a popup opener, and a reason on a disabled control. A plan that adds or redraws a control binds those from the start (its `hint`, `title`, `help`, `requires` or `description` while disabled; `expanded` once `openMenu` takes it), whether or not that sweep has landed: `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/moves-without-dragging`'s move buttons, `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/git-panel-action-list`'s controls and commit menu, `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/name-and-chord-gaps`'s rail and dock close, `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/empty-state-copy`'s empty-state actions.
- **Edges decided against the census critic's list.** `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/chrome-type-scale` requires `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/accordion-styling-facts`, not the reverse: §2.2's new text and its Signals check are false until the part rename lands, while §5.2's token is right either way. `plan:studio/file-tree-command-records` requires `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/name-and-chord-gaps`, not the reverse, which would cycle: the records are drawn by `placementRows`, and the tree's hand rows keep their flags until then. `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/moves-without-dragging` and `plan:studio/file-tree-command-records` share the `entry` fact and land in either order, so neither requires the other. Two edges wait for a whole L plan though only its early slices are built on (`plan:studio-ui-guidelines/name-and-chord-gaps` and `plan:studio/file-tree-command-records` on GPA1.1 and GPA1.2 of `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/git-panel-action-list`); if GPA1.3 stalls, its welcome-screen work splits into a plan of its own and the edges move to the part left.
- **Editorial drift has an owner or a patch release.** §5.1's goes with `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/accordion-styling-facts`, §8.7's (the prompt's error region, the four layer hosts, the modal keyboard stand-down, the misplaced "Auto-hides" bullet) with `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/inline-error-role`, and §9.3's (the gone `panel-scheduler.ts`, the "about thirty" count) with `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/retire-renderer-registry`. No plan edits §8.4 or §9.4, so their drift waits for a patch release.
- **§13.3 is contradicted and carries no marker.** `plan:studio-ui-guidelines/inline-error-role` found that a failed `project.json` write is announced twice (an assertive Problem from `commitProjectConfig` in packages/studio/src/tabs/project-config.ts, and a `role="alert"` line in five settings sections), against rule 3. By the open-item rule above it needs a marker and a plan; neither exists yet, and that plan's `ALERT_PARTS` names the lines so whichever plan takes it edits one list.
