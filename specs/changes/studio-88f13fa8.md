§7.1, §22.7: the Styles workspace ships with three views of project.json, Elements (stylebook), Variables (tokens, the CSS custom-property editor moved out of Project Settings, its rows named by their real --names) and Code; styles.open is a scripted alias of view.setWorkspace with an optional view, not a palette row; the Outline follows the view and points the Variables view at a property; the Variables view states that its edits save as they are made and that an undo waits for Save; the Style tab beside it says where the work is; §17.1's Moved footnote opens the Variables view.
