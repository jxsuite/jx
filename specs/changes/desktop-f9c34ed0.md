ElectroBun is removed and the Electron application is the one desktop framework: section 7 becomes the packaging chapter (electron-builder, the staged backend and its smoke test, self-updates through electron-updater, signing and the release lanes), the shared services server and the updater, window and canvas-URL RPC requests are gone, the Windows installer is NSIS with a Store appx in place of the MSI, Linux ships an AppImage, and section 9.3 graduates.
